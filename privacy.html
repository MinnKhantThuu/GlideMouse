Privacy

Last updated: 7 October 2026

English

GlideMouse has no advertising, analytics, tracking SDKs or developer-run accounts. Mouse events, app-specific mappings and settings are processed and stored on your Mac. They are not sent to the developer. Keyboard shortcuts are recorded only when you explicitly start shortcut recording.

Diagnostics remain local unless you choose to export and share them. Exported files may include macOS version, device names, app identifiers and mouse-event details. Review a file before sharing it. Importing and exporting settings only reads or writes files you choose.

The direct-download edition can check for updates through Sparkle using GitHub-hosted files. GitHub receives normal network request information such as your IP address. You can turn automatic checks off in Settings. The Mac App Store edition uses Apple’s update system and contains no Sparkle updater.

Email, website and support links open services you choose to visit. Those services have their own privacy policies. To remove local settings, quit the app and remove its settings folder from Application Support (or its macOS sandbox container for the Store edition).

简体中文

GlideMouse 不包含广告、分析或跟踪 SDK，也不要求开发者账户。鼠标事件、按应用设置和偏好仅在你的 Mac 上处理及保存，不会发送给开发者。只有你主动开始录制时才记录键盘快捷键。

诊断文件保存在本地，仅在你主动导出并分享时才离开设备。文件可能包含 macOS 版本、设备名称、应用标识和鼠标事件详情，请在分享前检查。导入和导出仅操作你选择的文件。

直接下载版本通过 Sparkle 检查 GitHub 托管的更新。GitHub 会收到 IP 地址等常规网络请求信息；你可以在设置中关闭自动检查。Mac App Store 版本仅使用 Apple 的更新系统，不包含 Sparkle。

邮件、网站和支持链接会打开你选择访问的服务，其隐私政策另行适用。联系开发者 Minn Khant Thu：minnkhantthu.ucsy@gmail.com。
